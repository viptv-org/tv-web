# VIPTV branding asset source

`assets/brand/viptv-v.svg` is the VIPTV V for every surface: the branded V of the Roku wordmark as one closed path, traced from the wordmark and reproducible with `assets/brand/tools/trace_v.py`. Its ink box is the SVG's viewBox and 1 unit is 1 px of `assets/roku/roku/images/viptv-mark.png`, the wordmark's V crop. Geometry, colours, the trace method, pinned tools and hashes are in [the asset manifest](../assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md#branded-v-master-app-icon-and-tv-banner).

`assets/roku/roku/images/viptv-wordmark.png` is the checked-in master for the Roku VIPTV V mark, splash and channel poster, and the image the V was traced from. Its SHA-256 is `4d6d473c5ba0f9ab6cf7b26a36f8fa85da3abeb543817480a2af6512b47c8f7b`. The derived mark, splash, and Roku channel poster are listed in [the asset manifest](../assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md).

To regenerate derived Roku branding, run `npm install --prefix assets/roku --no-audit --no-fund`, then `node assets/roku/roku/tools/make_brand_assets.cjs`. This operation reads the preserved wordmark and rewrites only `viptv-mark.png`, `splash-hd.jpg`, and `channel-poster-hd.jpg` in the Roku design asset copy. Verify the three SHA-256 values against an updated manifest before publishing an asset sync.

The script's `--extract` path is retained as historical provenance only: it required the pre-extraction original image, which is not an authoritative design input. Do not extract from a generated splash. The baseline process and restriction are recorded in the [Roku source and build instructions](https://github.com/viptv-org/roku/blob/a047d9ca5fc80898013eefb66120d20fab5048c0/roku/README.md).

The square launcher icon, the adaptive and monochrome layers, the in-app mark tile and the 320×180 TV banner are generated from the vector master by `assets/brand/tools/make_app_icon.py` into `assets/brand/`. Each of them places the master's path; none types a V glyph. `scripts/validate.py` re-renders them from the master and fails when a committed file no longer matches, so a stale icon cannot ship. The app copies two of them byte for byte — the 1024 px square as its desktop window and launcher icon, the banner as its TV banner — and records that pin in its own `androidApp/src/main/LAUNCHER_ASSETS.md`. See [the asset manifest](../assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md#branded-v-master-app-icon-and-tv-banner) for sizes and commands.
